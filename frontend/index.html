    How TravelJioo supports your agency

    Plain-language overview of B2B travel CRM capabilities. The phone preview and this site use simulated demo data—not live bookings, GDS search, or production integrations unless you connect them on crm.ratehonk.com.

    • Smart Flight Systems

      Plan flight segments inside itineraries and walk the booking workflow in the CRM preview—search here is illustrative, not a live airline feed.

      • Multi-city flight legs in itinerary builder
      • Lead-to-proposal booking stages on deals
      • Demo voucher compile (flight segment copy)
      • PDF travel vouchers & exports
    • Hotel Intelligence Hub

      Attach hotel stays to tour quotes and review accommodation context on deals—room counts and rates in the demo are sample lines, not live availability.

      • Hotel lines in multi-city quotes
      • Package tours with stay details (demo)
      • Flight & hotel voucher compilation step
      • Supplier invoice & receipt records
    • Dynamic Package Builder

      Compose tour packages with value, routes, and documents—what you see in the phone preview is a structured demo package, not a customer booking.

      • Tour package value on inbound leads
      • Itinerary builder with PDF export
      • Multi-city flight & hotel composition
      • Confirmed booking & payment demo flow
    • Group Travel Engine

      Keep tour and group inquiries organized with traveler context on each deal—this showcase uses a single-traveler demo, not a live passenger manifest.

      • Traveler name & trip on lead cards
      • Pipeline stages for group-style tours
      • Follow-up tasks across deal stages
      • Booking confirmation & receipt logging
    • Business Intelligence Pro

      See pipeline value, deal counts, and commission-oriented revenue views—dashboard numbers in the preview are illustrative demo metrics.

      • Pipeline value & open deals (demo KPIs)
      • Agent commission tracking
      • Revenue analytics for tour operators
      • Real-time lead & deal pipeline tracking
    • Enterprise Integrations

      Connect agency workflows using documented platform interfaces—only list integrations your deployment actually enables; this page does not enable them for you.

      • REST API (documented product stack)
      • PDF, CSV & invoice export formats
      • Multi-currency payment processing support
